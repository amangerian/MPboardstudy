Wizard's Escape

Cast fast · Cast true · Get out

The situation

You are locked in a haunted castle. Every room's exit is blocked by three identical hiding places — you cannot tell them apart by looking, only by the answer written on each. Shatter the right one and the way opens. Miss and the spell fizzles: no points lost, but the clock keeps running, and a slow spell is a weak spell.
